WHERE RECOVERY CAN LEAD

Recovery is the beginning of what comes next.

These are the outcomes the foundation is working toward. Select a card to learn more.

Healthier Lives

Long-term recovery involves the whole person. The foundation's work is aimed at connecting people with resources that support physical, mental and emotional well-being alongside practical assistance.

About the foundation →

Stronger Families

Recovery affects families too. Stability, employment and community connection can help people rebuild relationships and strengthen the support systems around them.

Stable Futures

Housing stability is one of the practical barriers that can make rebuilding life after treatment harder. Housing-related help is part of what the foundation is designing, subject to funding and approval.

View program areas →

Meaningful Opportunities

Employment, job readiness, training and education can be strong foundations for independence. These are program areas the foundation is developing.

Thriving Communities

When people in recovery are supported, communities benefit. The foundation intends to work with partners who share that goal.
