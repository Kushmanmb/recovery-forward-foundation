OUR PURPOSE

Recovery Should Lead Somewhere.

Recovery Forward Foundation is being established to help people move from recovery into long-term stability, independence, and productive participation in their communities. Recovery is not simply the completion of treatment. Our purpose is to help bridge the gap between treatment and sustainable independence.

Our Mission

Recovery Forward Foundation exists to help individuals build stable and independent lives following substance-use recovery by connecting verified recovery milestones with practical assistance, opportunity, and long-term community support.

How the Proposed Program Works

Referral or Application

An individual is referred or submits an application for assistance.

Begin application →

Registration

The application is registered and assigned a randomized RCGI ID for tracking, with no personal information required.

Recovery Milestone Pending

The participant continues their recovery journey and works toward the required milestone.

Independent Verification

Approved verifiers confirm milestone completion through a secure process.

Eligibility Review

The application is reviewed to confirm eligibility for grant consideration.

Grant Application and Review

The final application is reviewed and approved, or declined, for funding.

Approval or Decline

The applicant is notified of the decision. If approved, funding is prepared for support services.

Assistance

Funds may be released for approved needs such as housing, transportation, work tools, education and other essentials.

Assistance depends on approval and available funding.

Recordkeeping and Audit

Financial activity is intended to be tracked with transparency and audited to support accountability.

Transparency →

Privacy and Accountability

The Foundation intends to protect participant dignity through minimal information collection, randomized participant identifiers, independent verification, separation of financial responsibilities, and documented oversight.

Personal recovery or medical information should not be published on public websites or blockchains.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Six commitments

No Personal Information

The foundation intends not to collect names, addresses, Social Security numbers or other unnecessary identifying information.

Anonymous Application

Applicants receive a randomized RCGI ID to track their application while keeping their identity private.

Independent Verification

Recovery milestones are intended to be confirmed by approved, independent verifiers rather than self-verified.

Transparent Records

Status updates, verifications and approvals are intended to be documented using privacy-conscious records.

Financial Accountability

Financial responsibilities are intended to be separated, with documented oversight of how funds are used.

Community Trust

Privacy, transparency and accountability are meant to strengthen trust among participants, supporters and the community.
